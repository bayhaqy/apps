🍎 Install on iOS (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with up-arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The app icon will appear on your home screen.
  5. Launch from the home screen — it runs full-screen and works offline.

🤖 Install on Android (Chrome/Edge)

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on Android.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm — the app installs and works offline.
