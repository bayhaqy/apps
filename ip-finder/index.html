Network · GeoIP

IP Location Finder

Auto-detect your public IP on load, or look up any IPv4 / IPv6 to see country, city, GPS coordinates, ISP, and ASN on an interactive OpenStreetMap. Three providers with automatic fallback (ipwho.is → ipapi.co → geojs.io) so lookups keep working even when one provider is rate-limited. Tip: mobile-carrier, VPN, or Cloudflare IPs often resolve to the carrier core / exit node city — not your physical location. This is a known IP-geolocation limit.
