How to use

Two modes: generate a QR code from your content, or scan one with your camera or an image file.

01

Pick a content type

Choose Text, URL, Wi-Fi, Email, Phone, SMS, or vCard from the row of buttons at the top.

02

Fill the form

The input fields adapt to the type you chose. The preview updates live as you type.

03

Customize (optional)

Change colors, size, error-correction level, and margin. Higher error correction lets the QR survive smudges.

04

Export

Download as PNG or SVG, or copy the PNG to your clipboard to paste into a document or image editor.

05

Scan

Switch to the Scan tab, allow camera access, point at a QR code. Or upload an image to scan instead.
