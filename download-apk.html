Get these apps on your phone

The recommended way to install this app on your phone is as a Progressive Web App (PWA) — it works on both Android and iOS, requires no APK sideloading, and runs 100% offline after first load.

🤖 Android (Chrome / Edge)

  • Open https://bayhaqy.my.id/apps/ in Chrome or Edge.
  • Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right.
  • Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Confirm — the app installs and works offline.

🍎 iOS (Safari)

  • Open https://bayhaqy.my.id/apps/ in Safari.
  • Tap the Share button (square with up-arrow).
  • Choose Add to Home Screen.
  • Tap Add — the icon appears on your home screen.
Note on .apk: A standalone Android .apk is not yet published. The PWA install above provides the same offline experience on both Android and iOS without sideloading. If you specifically need a native .apk build, follow the instructions in APK_BUILD_GUIDE.md to wrap this site with Capacitor.
