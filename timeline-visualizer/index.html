How to export your Timeline
  1. On your phone, open Google Maps → your profile picture → Your data in Maps → Download your Timeline data, or export from takeout.google.com.
  2. You get a Timeline.json (newest), Records.json, location-history.json (iPhone) or a Takeout .zip — any of them works.
  3. Drop the file here. The whole .zip is fine too — we find the right file automatically.
  4. Press play and watch your journey animate.

100% private — parsing and animation happen entirely in your browser. Your location data never leaves your device.
