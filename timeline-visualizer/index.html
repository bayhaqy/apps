How to export your Timeline
  1. Open takeout.google.com on your phone or desktop, or in Google Maps go to Your data in Maps → Download your Timeline data.
  2. Deselect all products, then select Maps (Timeline) — or Location History for older exports.
  3. Export and download the .zip file.
  4. Drop the whole .zip here — we find the right file automatically. Or extract it and drop Timeline.json, Records.json or Location History.json.

🔒 100% private — parsing happens entirely in your browser. Your location data never leaves your device.
