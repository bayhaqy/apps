Why PWA instead of an APK? A Progressive Web App (PWA) gives you the same full-screen experience as a native app, but installs directly from your browser — no APK sideloading, no unsigned-file warnings, and you always get the latest version automatically. It works perfectly on Android 14 and iOS.

Android install

Chrome, Edge, or Samsung Internet — Android 8+ including Android 14

  1. Open https://bayhaqy.my.id/apps/ in Chrome (or your preferred browser).
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  4. Confirm by tapping "Install" in the dialog.
  5. The app now appears in your app drawer — launch it for a full-screen experience with no browser chrome.

iOS install

Safari only — iOS 12+ including iOS 17

  1. Open https://bayhaqy.my.id/apps/ in Safari (other browsers won't work for iOS install).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with up-arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
  5. The app icon appears on your home screen — launch it for a full-screen experience.

Frequently asked questions

I downloaded an APK earlier and it wouldn't install on Android 14. Why?
The previous APK file was a placeholder package — it wasn't a properly signed Android app, so Android 14 (which enforces strict signing and target-SDK rules) rejected it. The correct, reliable way to install is to use "Add to Home screen" from Chrome, which installs a Progressive Web App (PWA). PWAs work perfectly on Android 14 and give you the same full-screen, app-like experience as a native APK.
Does the app work offline?
Most tools (PDF editor, hasher, JWT decoder, metronome, tuner, encryptor, etc.) run entirely client-side once the page is loaded. A few tools that call external APIs (DNS Lookup, IP Location Finder) require an internet connection. Your browser will cache the app shell so it launches quickly even on a slow connection.
Is this the same as a native Android app?
Functionally, yes — full-screen, launches from the home screen, no browser UI. The difference is it's powered by your browser's rendering engine rather than native Android code, which means it's always up-to-date and works identically on iOS, Android, and desktop.
How much storage does it use?
Less than 5 MB for the app shell. Heavy libraries (pdf-lib, pdfjs, signature_pad) are loaded on-demand only when you use the relevant tool. The AI Background Remover model (~80 MB) is cached only if you use that tool.
Can I uninstall it later?
Yes — long-press the app icon on your home screen and drag it to "Uninstall" or "Remove", just like any other app. This removes the icon and clears the cached data.
