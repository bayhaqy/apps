How to use

Five steps. Everything runs in your browser — no account, no sync, no upload.

01

Create a vault

Click "Create new vault" and pick a master password. This is the only password you must remember. Add an optional hint in case you forget.

02

Add entries

Click "Add entry" and fill in title, username, password, URL, and notes. Use the generator panel for strong passwords.

03

Use day-to-day

Search, copy username or password (auto-clears clipboard after 30 seconds), reveal with the eye icon, or edit and delete entries.

04

Export a backup

Click "Export" to download the encrypted vault as a JSON file. Store it somewhere safe — it requires your master password to decrypt.

05

Lock when done

Click "Lock" or just walk away — the vault auto-locks after 5 minutes of inactivity. You can change this in the top bar.
